# Endless Chest

**Endless Chest** is a Minecraft Fabric mod that adds a special chest with virtually unlimited storage and the ability to transfer items between chests.

Organize your items, search your storage, and build a connected storage network across your Minecraft world.

## Features

- **Unlimited Storage** – Store large amounts of items in a single chest.
- **Search & Sorting** – Quickly find and organize stored items.
- **Page Navigation** – Browse your entire storage inventory.
- **Named Chests** – Every chest receives a unique random name that you can customize.
- **Item Transfers** – Send items directly to other Endless Chests.
- **Saved Destinations** – Add, browse, and remove frequently used recipients.
- **Persistent Storage** – Chest contents and network information are saved with your world.
- **Crafting Range** - Crafting directly from the endless chest in adjustable radius.
- **Custom Design** – Unique textures and an animated chest lid.
- **Multiplayer Support** – Designed for Fabric servers and singleplayer worlds.
- **Language Support** – English and German.

## How to Use

1. Craft and place an Endless Chest.
2. Open it and store your items.
3. Rename your chest to something memorable, such as `Main Storage` or `Mining Base`.
4. Add another chest's name to your saved destinations.
5. Select an item, choose the amount, and send it to your destination chest.

**Note:** Destination chests must be loaded and available to receive items.

## Installation

1. Install **Fabric Loader** for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Install the required **Fabric API**.
3. Place the Endless Chest `.jar` file in your `mods` folder.
4. Launch Minecraft.

## Configuration

The mod includes configurable options, including its crafting recipe.
